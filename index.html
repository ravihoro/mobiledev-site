Dotify

Always-on display & dot icons

Your screen and icons, in glowing dots.

A free always-on display for Android. See the time, date and battery in a dot matrix face whenever your screen turns off, then give your app icons the same look.

  • Static and animated dot matrix faces
  • Draw your own, or turn a photo into dots
  • Dot icons for your home screen, recolored or hand-drawn
  • Smart about your battery

Cineframe

65:24 panoramic camera

Shoot ultra-wide. Frame it like cinema.

A minimalist camera for photos and videos in the 65:24 panoramic format, with a cinematic film-camera feel. Compose in the frame as you shoot, with no cropping in post.

  • True 65:24 photos and video
  • Live cinematic LUTs, grain, vignette and halation
  • RAW (DNG) capture and manual control
  • HEVC or H.264 video at your chosen frame rate
